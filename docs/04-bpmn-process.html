THREADLINE POS — Business Process Model (BPMN)

As-built process model of the running apparel EPOS · BPMN 2.0 notation · companion to docs/01-workflow.md

Four processes make up the software: a manager sets up what can be sold, then the shop floor runs sales and returns against it, and the day is closed off with a cash reconciliation. Pools are participants, lanes are roles, rounded boxes are tasks (person = someone does it, gear = the POS does it automatically), diamonds are decisions, circles are start/end events, and dashed arrows are messages between participants.

1 · Catalog setup — Back Office

Manager-only. Nothing can be sold until a Style, its size×colour Variants and a price exist. Promotions are optional, date-windowed rules layered on top.

2 · Sale

The main flow. Price, promotion and GST are computed by the system from the tax-inclusive MRP. Payment authorisation is a message exchange with the PSP; nothing is committed (receipt, stock, ledger) until the payment is approved.

3 · Return & Exchange

Exchange-first: the default path swaps size/colour and moves money only for a price difference. A refund goes back to the original tender. Over ₹2,000, or outside the 30-day window, needs a manager. The original sale is never edited — a reversal is posted.

4 · Cash management — End of day

A blind denomination count is compared with the system's expected figure. Inside ±₹100 it closes straight through; outside, a typed reason and a manager sign-off are required before the Z-report.
